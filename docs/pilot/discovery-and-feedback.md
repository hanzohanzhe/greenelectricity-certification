# Pilot discovery and feedback

Ask the prospective site:

- Which meter measures rooftop generation, import/export and each tenant?
- What are the asset and tenancy boundaries?
- What interval, timezone, coverage and missing-data markers are available?
- Is allocation physical, contractual, pro-rata or priority-based?
- Which fields and aggregates may be made public?
- Which objective fact matters most: onsite share, temporal match, source
  lineage, location, commissioning date or something else?
- Who will rely on the proof and what decision will they make?
- Would they provide a sample month and join a structured pilot?

After the demo record whether the participant can correctly explain generation,
onsite consumption and export; understands the tenant rule; trusts the source
presentation; sees commercial value; will provide real interval data; would pay,
co-develop or introduce another site; and which concept was hardest.
